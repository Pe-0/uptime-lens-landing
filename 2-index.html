Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Built for small teams who want monitoring that just works — no agents, no YAML, no PhD.

30-second checks

HTTP(S), keyword and ping monitors from 6 regions, with false-positive protection — we confirm from 2 regions before alerting.

Instant alerts

Email, Slack, Discord, WhatsApp and webhooks. Escalation rules so the right person gets woken up, not the whole team.

Public status pages

A beautiful status page for your customers in one click, on your own subdomain. Incidents post themselves.

SSL & domain expiry

We watch your certificates and domain renewals too, and warn you 30, 14 and 3 days before anything expires.

Response-time analytics

P50/P95 latency graphs per region. Spot the slow endpoint before it becomes the down endpoint.

Developer-friendly API

Create monitors, pull incidents and mute alerts from a clean REST API. Full OpenAPI spec included.

How it works

Live in under two minutes

Add a URL

Paste your website or API endpoint. Pick a check interval and regions. That's the whole setup.

We watch it

UptimeLens probes it around the clock, tracks response times, SSL, and keyword presence.

You get alerted

The second a check fails from two regions, you're notified on the channels you chose. Recovery alerts too.
